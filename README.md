# musicZM — Complete Android Music & Video Player (Phases 1–10)

**musicZM** is a modern Android application built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose**, **Material 3**, **Media3 ExoPlayer**, **Room Database**, and **DataStore**.

## Included Features (All Phases Complete)
- **Phase 1 & 2:** Up-to-date Kotlin DSL Gradle setup, 12 clean architecture packages, adaptive vector **MZ AI** launcher icon, animated Splash Screen, and Royal Purple / Electric Blue / Cyan / Soft Pink / Lavender Light & Dark themes.
- **Phase 3:** Home Dashboard (Greeting, Search bar, Recently Played, Most Played, Recently Added, Favorites, Music & Video Categories, Continue Playing, Mini-Player) + 5-tab Bottom Navigation.
- **Phase 4:** Automatic MediaStore Audio Scanner with 8 tabs (All Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders, Recently Played, Recently Added, Favorites, Playlists).
- **Phase 5:** Background Media3 `MediaSessionService`, Lock-screen & Notification controls, Full-screen Music Player with rotating album disc, shuffle, repeat, speed, and volume.
- **Phase 6:** MediaStore Video Scanner & Full HD Video Player with landscape toggle, brightness & volume sliders, 10s seek, playback speed, subtitles toggle, Picture-in-Picture (PiP), and auto-resume playback.
- **Phase 7:** Room Database Playlist & Favorites system (Create, Rename, Delete, Play All, Add/Remove songs, Reorder songs, Favorite Playlists).
- **Phase 8:** Unified Search across Songs, Artists, Albums, Videos, Playlists, and Folders.
- **Phase 9:** DataStore Settings (Light/Dark mode, 3 Brand Accent Palettes, Language selector, Gapless & Resume toggles) + Hardware Audio Equalizer (Presets, 5-Band EQ, Bass Boost, Virtualizer).
- **Phase 10:** Android 13+ & Legacy runtime permission handler, ProGuard release rules, and APK build readiness.

## How to Build in Android Studio
1. Extract `musicZM-complete.zip`.
2. Open Android Studio -> **Open** -> select the `musicZM` directory.
3. Wait for Gradle sync to finish.
4. Click **Build > Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) > Build APK(s)** to generate your installable `.apk`.
